Evidence

Full review with revision-pinned source citations and license details

Parallel macOS filesystem traversal builds a folder-local, memory-mapped name index; FSEvents supplies changes. An immutable-segment content index combines deduplicated content IDs, trigram posting lists and Bloom filters before rereading candidate files. A single-writer daemon shares results with CLI, stdio and Unix-socket clients.
