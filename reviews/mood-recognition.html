Key points

What matters most, in order.

1

Behavior, not mind reading

Pointer, click, scroll and typing-event patterns produce friction labels. No camera, microphone, facial analysis or text sentiment is described. The public showcase uses scripted scores, not live inference.

2

80% is planted-event detection

The publisher reports 48 of 60 planted problems caught across more than ten people, with 2.3% of calm time flagged. This is not 80% mood accuracy, and no independent replication or demographic/generalization breakdown was found.

3

Local does not mean nothing retained

The documented personal pace baseline persists in localStorage by default; memory-only mode is optional. Model-runtime egress is claimed absent, but the vendor website has separate page-view/account scripts. Current model and kit downloads are not MIT.

Evidence, not a demonstration benchmark

Training reportedly combines 80 simulated people and real sessions from more than ten people. Participant-held-out scoring is encouraging, but exact recruitment, per-class metrics, calibration and independent outcomes are missing. Short searches can be missed; blocked checkout and normal exploration can be labeled lost.

Actual public implementation

The showcase defines a fixed 15.8-second pointer route and hard-coded BEATS. Public loader documentation describes tree traversal and softmax, but the current feature collector and model were not downloaded or audited. No source execution, live inference, uploads or device access occurred.

Privacy and consent

Documentation claims no text, values, screen, audio or video input. Behavioral baselines nevertheless persist locally by default. Expiry, deletion and training-participant consent are not established. No browser permission prompt is not consent to profiling; provide off/reset controls and avoid sensitive inputs and output export.

Separate service boundary

The product page includes GoatCounter and account scripts. Vendor policy lists Cloudflare, Resend, Stripe and Anthropic for the separate Studio Agent—not evidence of mood-data transfer to an LLM. This was a static inspection, not a network audit.

Verdict

The call, and why.

⚠️ Interesting

A compact local-model idea for optional, reversible help. Small first-party studies, account-gated runtime code and restrictive licensing prevent a production endorsement. Fix known UI obstacles first; never treat labels as facts about a person.
