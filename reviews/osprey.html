Benchmark transparency

24 cases with output oracles and repeated timings. Tracked baselines mix dates/machines and use an earlier Osprey compiler. No general Rust/C parity follows; no benchmark was rerun.

Reviewed v0.32.0 at 6ad8700e39dff01391318d2223207f22b7b2a8b3. The sandbox and package capability map omit env. The Node service inherits environment, logs source/output, lacks application auth and accumulates unbounded output. Non-root containers/timeouts do not establish tenant isolation.
