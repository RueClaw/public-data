Key points

What matters, in order.

1

The object is the explanation

Don't draw boxes and arrows — invent a physical machine whose working is the concept. A cache key becomes a pin-tumbler lock; one GPU context becomes a gantry plotter visiting 16 wells. Every number shown comes from the same model that moves the parts.

2

An audit that proves the geometry

build.mjs --audit verifies no part passes through another — at rest, apart, and in every replayed frame — plus mounts, bend quality, and depth order. Verified locally on the Raptor engine: 80 tubes, 267 solids, 1,119 depth states, 1.7M overlap checks, passed.

3

WebGL inside the drawing

Fire, water, caustics and lamp light render in a canvas between back and front SVG layers, in the figure's own isometric camera — effects live inside the illustration, and the SVG still reads without WebGL.

4

Honest engineering

Zero dependencies, committed built pages, CI rebuilding every example, evals shipped in-repo, 3D mode labeled beta, a documented limits section. Verified builds: dial-indicator 0.13 s, desk-computer 0.14 s.
