Evidence

Full review with revision-pinned source citations and license details

Python scenes and mobjects feed a maturin-built Rust/PyO3 engine with wgpu rendering, Typst/mitex typesetting, x264 video and FFmpeg audio. Film records views and content-keyed geometry, coalescing identical consecutive frames. Separate Pyodide and WebGPU builds support a browser-worker authoring/player path.

Verdict
