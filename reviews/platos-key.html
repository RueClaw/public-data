Key points

What matters most, in order.

1

Functional transfer, not merely similar geometry

Authors report 41% median cross-model transfer versus 55% self-swap on facts both models already recall. This redirects access to known facts; it is not new knowledge installation.

2

Layer choice and scale matter

Selecting layers by geometric similarity yields 4.5% median transfer. Centering, rotation and recipient-scale matching are fitted on training facts; closed form does not mean calibration-free.

3

Composition is promising; universality remains open

Two-hop maps retain 99% median direct accuracy, and 256 PCs retain 74% of full-map accuracy. Neither figure measures end-to-end speedup or establishes a universal latent interface.

Method and controls

A donor last-subject-token state is centered, mapped by an SVD-derived orthogonal or semi-orthogonal transform, globally rescaled and shifted to the recipient mean. The scale is recipient centered Frobenius norm divided by donor centered Frobenius norm. Both models stay frozen; fewer than 5,000 known training facts fit the map.

Layer pairs maximize validation transfer, then evaluate subject-held-out facts. Self-swap controls intervention difficulty; random and shuffled maps test arbitrary alignment. Alternative map comparisons use the selected layers, not necessarily each alternative’s optimal layers.

Reported outcomes, not reproduced

Median transfer is 41% (34–46%) against 55% self-swap. Best controls per pair span 0.6–2.2%. Forty of 47 chains retain at least 95% of direct-map accuracy. PCA retention is 4%, 74% and 87% at 16, 256 and 512 components.

Dark-memory denominator

Of 2,600 donor-known recipient misses, 1,463 pass alternative recall or recognition tests; 267 recover under donor-key insertion. Independent arithmetic gives 18.25% of the qualifying subset, or 10.27% of all collected misses—not an overall benchmark improvement.

Reproduction gap

The retrieved article links background papers and VecMap, but no dedicated experiment code, paper, fact list, checkpoint manifest or raw results. Exact splits, prompts, answer grading, baseline tuning and uncertainty are insufficiently specified to reproduce the numbers.

Boundaries of the conclusion

Pairwise known-fact filtering and subject-held-out evaluation do not establish unseen-relation, multilingual, post-trained-model or reasoning generalization. The all-model shared-space animation is explicitly idealized, not a measured joint alignment.

Dark memories are operational

Paraphrase, three-example prompting or recognition among up to 50 candidates qualifies a miss. This differs from Inside-Out’s internal-versus-external answer-ranking framework and does not uniquely locate stored information or exclude answer-bearing donor features.

Practical next step

Use rotation plus scale as a research baseline, with functional layer selection and cost-matched donor-answer/text-transfer comparisons. Do not infer deployment readiness, inference savings or safety-compatible latent communication.

Verdict

The call, and why.

⚠️ Interesting but verify

A useful research baseline for interpretability and model stitching. Reported transfer, chaining and compression support a narrow functional alignment result; missing experiment artifacts and limited task coverage prevent claims of general knowledge transfer or cheaper inference.
