For those asking for the prompt, you can use this

Remove my household's personal information from every data broker and people-search site I can reach, not a sample. The goal is fewer spam calls, texts, and emails. Prioritize brokers that sell data to other brokers, but cover all of them.

PEOPLE (repeat for each person)
- Full legal name, plus nicknames, maiden names, and other spellings: [ ]
- Date of birth: [ ]
- Current address: [ ]
- Previous cities/addresses (last 10–15 years): [ ]
- Phone numbers, and whose each one is: [ ]
- Email to use for requests: [ ] (a separate email per person if possible, since some sites allow only one identity per email)
- State of residence: [ ] (cite that state's privacy and data broker laws)

BUILD THE MASTER LIST
1. Download the complete state data broker registries: California (http://cppa.ca.gov, downloadable with privacy emails and opt-out links) plus Texas, Vermont, and Oregon where possible. Add every people-search site you can find from current opt-out guides.
2. Remove duplicates, personal Gmail/Yahoo addresses, and marketing agencies. Save it as a spreadsheet on your computer.
3. Sort every entry into one of two groups: web opt-out form, or email-only.

WORK THE WHOLE LIST
4. Web forms: in batches of 10–15, search each site for each person's records and submit delete plus do-not-sell/share requests. Keep going until every form site is done.
5. Email-only: draft a deletion and opt-out request citing my state's law that lists everyone above. Send it in batches of about 40–50 recipients per email until every address has been covered. Show me each email as a draft first; I'll press Send.
6. Confirmations: watch my inbox, click every verification link, and finish any form that a reply points to. Brokers that say "use our web form" move to the form list.

RULES
- No ID uploads, no SSN (not even the last 4), no payments, no password accounts, no phone calls. Skip anything that needs those and list it for me.
- Never reply to a broker or email anyone new without showing me a draft first.
- Ask me only for things only I can do (texted codes, an email address for another person).

TRACK AND FOLLOW UP
7. Keep a tracker with each broker and each person's status: submitted, confirmed, no record, bounced, skipped (and why), or needs me.
8. After each round, give me a running total. Keep going in rounds until the list is empty or I say stop.
9. Set up follow-up checks for tomorrow and again in 3–4 weeks to recheck the people-search listings, click new links, tally replies and bounces, and re-submit anything that came back.
