## Text

The method comes from "Sparks of Weight-Space Programming: LLMs can natively read & write neural network weights" by four MIT CSAIL researchers, published November 2, 2026:

- Rikiya Takehi (joint first author)
- Jayden Teoh (joint first author)
- Alex Zhang
- Omar Khattab, whose group it is; he's also known for DSPy and ColBERT.

This method immediately inspired us to work to solve a long-standing problem with Qwen models: thinking often outruns the context window. 

Would it be possible for us to directly edit the weights so a model thought less, while retaining its intelligence?

The answer: yes.

Overall, this reduces the tokens used on GPQA Diamond by 30% while retaining accuracy.
