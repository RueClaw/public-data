## Text

10 GitHub repos that will be worth more than your degree in 2026.

Save this before you pay another $200K in tuition.

1. developer-roadmap
The exact skills, tools, and career paths for every tech role. 347K+ stars and the most bookmarked learning repo on GitHub.
Repo → https://github.com/kamranahmedse/developer-roadmap

2. freeCodeCamp
A full-stack development and machine learning curriculum with real certifications. Over 100K people have landed their first dev job through it.
Repo → https://github.com/freeCodeCamp/freeCodeCamp

3. coding-interview-university
A complete computer science study plan. The author used it to go from no CS degree to a software engineer role at Amazon.
Repo → https://github.com/jwasham/coding-interview-university

4. system-design-primer
The guide FAANG engineers use to prep for system design interviews. Real case studies, diagrams, and practice questions included.
Repo → https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer

5. build-your-own-x
Tutorials to build your own Git, Docker, database, or operating system from scratch. The best way to actually understand how software works.
Repo → https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x

6. project-based-learning
Real projects you can build in every major language. The fastest path from tutorial to portfolio.
Repo → https://github.com/practical-tutorials/project-based-learning

7. public-apis
330+ free APIs sorted by category. The fuel that turns every tutorial into a shippable project.
Repo → https://github.com/public-apis/public-apis

8. the-algorithms
Every algorithm implemented in every major language. The single best way to prep for technical interviews.
Repo → https://github.com/TheAlgorithms/Python

9. awesome-llm-apps
The modern AI engineer stack. The exact skills every AI job description asks for in 2026.
Repo → https://github.com/Shubhamsaboo/awesome-llm-apps

10. awesome-claude-code
The playbook for Claude Code, the tool 46% of developers now rate as their favorite.
Repo → https://github.com/hesreallyhim/awesome-claude-code

Here's the wildest part:

A CS degree costs $100K to $200K and takes 4 years.

Work through these 10 repos seriously and you will have the same knowledge, real projects to show, and interview prep that top tech companies actually screen for.

The difference is the paper and the debt.

A degree teaches you what a textbook wants you to know. These repos teach you what a paycheck wants you to know.

Save this before you forget.

100% free. 100% open source.
