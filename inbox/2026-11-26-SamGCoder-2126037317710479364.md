## Text

I wanted to see how close I could get to a screenshot from GTA 6 using my own Rust + Vulkan renderer.

This is a procedurally generated Miami running on my 1:1 scale Earth.

All of the buildings, cars, boats and bridges are generated directly in Rust, including the geometry for individual floors, balconies, windows and rooftops.

I'm actually using the same RTX impostor technique I developed for rendering millions of trees to render the entire city. Up close you get the real geometry, and in the distance a custom RTX intersection shader ray marches the impostors.

Even the glass on distant buildings has ray traced reflections, and individual windows light up as the sun goes down.

The city has procedural streets, residential areas, highways, bridges, traffic and vegetation, all built around real terrain and coastline data.

Everything is hardware ray traced in real time on a single RTX 5080, including GI, shadows and reflections.
