## Text

This is definitely where we are going.

- I don't care how much code or what agents wrote as long as I can get my outcome to a "Done state"

- Outcome can be anything: perf changes, reduce slop, build a feature. If you define outcome for your agents clearly they will get it done

- Human attention is precious and will get more precious as we scale agents. Humans will only need to be interrupted by agents for the ambiguous decisions, taste/design choices etc

- Everything can be quickly prototyped and then also can be thrown away if it doesn't work. As a startup you should be ready to experiment with a lot of crazy and ambitious idea. 

- Testing and verification of behaviors becomes so much more important than traditional unit and integration tests

- You agents needs to be primed with the team context so that your agents can be a part of your team and don't digress too much from your team norms and practices
