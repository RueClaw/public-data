## Text

Sharing the thought process behind the architecture for Marin 535B-A23B, which finished pretraining on 18T tokens last week: https://openathena.ai/blog/marin-535b-architecture/

Earlier this year the Marin project had 6 full-time employees at Open Athena, a mix of AI veterans and people newer to the space, like me. I learned a lot from the team as an IC. Since then we got our B200 GPU stack running in a month, scaled up 300x on GPU to a 535 billion parameter model, publicly forecasted the run, live-streamed the entire thing, and made it through pretraining slightly better than predicted with zero instabilities.

In the blog I call out specific contributions on architecture- but the broader gratitude is to the ecosystem. 2 years ago this would have been inconceivable on a first large scale attempt. Now it’s possible, in large part due to published work from Llama, DeepSeek, Qwen, Gemma, Olmo, Cohere, Nemotron, Arcee, Inkling, Mistral, Microsoft AI, Kimi, Muse, academia, and many others. The recipes are out there. A small team with big dreams (and some funding) can train a big model. (And if you have doubts on that, you can copy our fully open recipe from data to code).

Of course, training a model does not produce a binary outcome. I am proud of how far we’ve come and optimistic about how much more there is to explore! The team has grown recently and will be taking the model through SFT and RL soon, sharing the findings along the way.
