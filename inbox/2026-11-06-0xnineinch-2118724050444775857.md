## Text

I turned Grok Bot into my personal money coach. its first finding: I was making spending decisions as if every week would be a $6,200 trading week

I checked my PnL constantly. upcoming bills, recurring charges, and the money I’d already committed barely got a look

so I gave it my transaction exports, trading report, and payment dates

the first review put three numbers together:

$6,200 earned
$1,940 spent
$4,260 remaining before upcoming bills and reserves

I had been treating that last number as money available to spend

Grok Bot built a calendar around it. every upcoming payment got an amount and a date. every recurring charge got flagged for review. trading capital stayed separate

now the weekly review answers:

→ what money actually came in?
→ what has already left?
→ what needs paying before the next income arrives?
→ which expenses keep growing?
→ what can I spend without touching money already committed?

before a larger purchase, I give it the price. it shows the effect on the next 30 days, including the quieter week I keep forgetting could happen

it also explains the decision. I can see why a purchase fits the plan or which payment it would squeeze

no dramatic “you’re banned from buying sneakers” messages. just my own numbers, with the consequences attached

every Friday, I get one report: what changed, what needs attention, and how much is genuinely available

apparently, the financial literacy lesson I needed was hiding in the transactions I never bothered to review
