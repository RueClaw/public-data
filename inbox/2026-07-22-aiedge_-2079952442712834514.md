Config. (turn on memory & skills)
One of the most powerful things about Hermes is its persistent memory.
It remembers anything and everything about you - far better than even tools like Claude.
To get the most out of your Composio setup, go into your configuration and make sure persistent memory and skill generation are both switched on.
A brief explanation of both:
Memory: Hermes recalls past conversations and context across sessions. 
(aka ask it about something you discussed last week, and it actually remembers, instead of starting from zero).
Skills: Every task Hermes completes gets saved as a reusable skill. The next time you ask for something similar, Hermes will use your saved SOP.

Scheduling Recurring Tasks
This is one of my favorite Hermes use cases, and I don't see people talking about it enough.
Hermes has a built-in cron scheduler that allows you to schedule tasks.
Things like:
"Every day at 9 am, scan my Slack and ping me with a summarized report."
"Before I go to bed, send me a quick update on my to-do list so I can set priorities for tomorrow." 
Interrupt Control
You can message Hermes mid-task to redirect it instead of waiting for it to finish. 
Use "/stop."
 
Step 5: Customizing Your Assistant 
Out of the box, Hermes is capable but generic. 
This step is where you actually shape it into something that feels like yours.
Personality Selection
Hermes isn't locked into one tone. You can swap between built-in personalities depending on what you're using it for, direct and no-nonsense for work, more relaxed for personal tasks.
Use "/personality direct."
Model Selection
This is one of the biggest factors for your agent's personality.
In my opinion, Claude models have the warmest personality (human-like responses), and GPT models are best if you want a robotic feel to your agent.
You can swap models anytime using "model provider:model" or by just asking your agent to switch in real time.
Set Home
Use "/sethome" to create a chat as your home channel.
Pets
This is a new feature. By using "/pet," you can assign your Hermes agent an avatar.
 
Discord Hubs 
One last thing. 
I've experimented around, and I've found that Discord is best if you want to set up a "hub" for your AI agents.
WhatsApp and Telegram are great for quick messaging, but with Discord, you can create various channels dedicated to various subagents and monitor them in real time. 
It provides a "home" like feel to Hermes, instead of just texting an AI agent, and it's also pretty cool to have a dedicated Discord server for your AI workflows.
