## Text

I started taking GlyNAC four years ago. After the first six months I stopped for a few months and saw health improvements disappear and quickly restarted and I’ve never stopped since.  

My protocol is slightly lower in dose than what was studied because I think 7g of NAC a day is a lot. I keep it to 4g of NAC. and for a long time 6-7g of Glycine daily. For those 55+ it’s a wonder “drug”.  Tell my friends Glutathione is like garbage collectors for your cells. And by 60 you don’t have enough of them. Take these two cheap amino acids in abundance and your body does the rest. 

With the latest info on Glycine I’ve recently went to 8 to 10g per day of it. Basically I grab a handful of 1000mg capsules and as long as it’s at least eight to ten, I’m good to go. Still 4g of NAC.

One benefit for me is taking my 135 / 89 blood pressure down to 115 / 77. But other blood markers have improved as well over the years.

I’ve now recommended it to half a dozen others (60+) and those who stick with it see good results as well. And my own doctor had her mother start taking it.  I couldn’t get my own mom to take it (too many pills for her).

For those under 55, you probably don’t need high dose. Take 2g of NAC and 4-5g of Glycine a day. But if you have a lot of mitochondrial dysfunction then go higher earlier.  

NOTE: if you take medication to lower blood pressure you will need to monitor blood pressure and reduce medication as your blood pressure improves. You will likely need to stop the medication after a few months. One friend I started on this journey got dangerous low blood pressure because his doctor insisted he stay on his medication. He quit the GlyNAC and listened to his doctor. Some people don’t understand.

In the last year and a half I’ve also added NAD+ daily injections (90mg).  For me the combo of the two is kinda like the fountain of youth. Or is at least keeping me looking and feeling like I’m healthy and maybe in my lower 50’s (I’m 63). No one guesses my age correctly the last couple of years.
