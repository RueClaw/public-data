TensorFold 1.2.7 is out. 🔥

A big fix for long conversations on Metal: Qwen3.8-27B now reuses its history even when the client drops earlier reasoning.

In the measured M3 Ultra test with 34k history and a 5 GiB prompt cache, follow-up time to first token dropped from about 85s to 0.3s. Same replies in that test.

Also in this release:

• Responses API over WebSocket.
• LightOnOCR-3 0.8B and 4B image chat on CUDA.
• Opt-in GLM conversation reuse at turn boundaries on CUDA.
• Prompt-cache and disk-tier stats in the dashboard.
• Targeted speed and memory improvements across Metal, CUDA and HIP.
• Fixes for GPU builds, malformed GLM tool calls, damaged JPEGs and disk-cache recovery.
